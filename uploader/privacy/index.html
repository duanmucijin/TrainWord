大神水印神器隐私政策

生效及更新日期：2026 年 9 月 6 日

本政策适用于“大神水印神器”的 iPhone、iPad 及 Apple 芯片 Mac 版本。

1. 我们处理的信息

  • 账号信息：您注册时设置的用户名、加密后的密码凭据、登录会话和账号标识。
  • 设备标识：随机生成或由系统提供的设备标识，用于游客身份、服务安全和额度管理。
  • 用户内容：您主动提交用于处理的链接或文本，以及相应的服务处理结果。
  • 购买信息：Apple 返回的商品、交易标识和订阅状态，用于开通、恢复和同步会员权益。我们不会获取完整银行卡信息。
  • 诊断信息：请求结果、错误原因、应用版本和必要的运行日志，用于排查故障和保障服务。

2. 相册与通知权限

只有在您保存导出内容时，应用才会申请“添加到照片”权限，用于把图片、视频、GIF 或实况照片写入系统相册。选择素材使用 Apple 系统选择器，应用不会因此读取您的整个照片图库。

只有在您主动开启“处理完成提醒”时，应用才会申请通知权限，用于告知视频处理完成或失败，不用于广告营销。只有在您主动拍摄照片或视频时，应用才会申请相机权限；录制视频并采集声音时会申请麦克风权限。

3. 信息的使用

上述信息仅用于提供账号登录、在线媒体处理、本地编辑、文件保存、会员权益、故障排查和安全防护。我们不会出售个人信息，也不会使用这些信息进行跨应用广告追踪。

4. 服务提供方

账号和业务接口可能由腾讯云相关服务承载；订阅支付由 Apple StoreKit 处理。在线素材处理会按照您的请求访问您提交链接所指向的来源平台。我们仅传输完成相应功能所必需的数据，并要求能够访问个人信息的服务提供方采取不低于本政策所述标准的保护措施。来源平台对其自身的数据处理适用其各自的隐私政策。

5. 保存期限与安全

账号和权益信息在账号存续期间保存；登录会话、故障日志和临时处理文件仅在功能、安全或排障所需期间保存，临时文件会按服务策略清理。密码不会以明文保存。法律另有要求的，按法定期限处理。

6. 您的选择与权利

您可以在系统设置中关闭相册或通知权限。登录账号后，可在 App 的“我的”页面发起永久注销；注销会删除账号资料、会话和权益关联。为满足财务、安全或法律义务而必须保留的交易记录会解除与已注销账号的直接关联，并仅在必要期限内保存。Apple 的购买记录由 Apple 管理，注销不会自动退款或取消自动续费，您可在 Apple 订阅设置中处理。

如需访问、更正、删除信息或提出隐私问题，请发送邮件至 jyinaune@163.com。

7. 第三方内容与儿童隐私

您应仅处理自己拥有权利或已获授权的内容，并遵守内容来源平台的规则。应用不以收集儿童个人信息为目的；未成年人应在监护人指导下使用。

8. 政策更新

功能或信息处理方式发生重大变化时，我们会更新本政策，并通过应用内页面或其他适当方式提示。

Watermark Master Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 6, 2026

This policy applies to Watermark Master on iPhone, iPad, and Mac computers with Apple silicon.

1. Information We Process

  • Account information: the username you choose, encrypted password credentials, sign-in sessions, and account identifiers.
  • Device identifiers: randomly generated or system-provided identifiers used for guest access, service security, and usage limits.
  • User content: links or text you submit for processing and the resulting service output.
  • Purchase information: product, transaction, and subscription status returned by Apple to activate, restore, and sync Premium access. We do not receive full payment-card details.
  • Diagnostic information: request results, error details, app version, and essential runtime logs used for troubleshooting and service security.

2. Photos, Camera, Microphone, and Notifications

The app requests permission to add to Photos only when you save exported images, videos, GIFs, or Live Photos. Media selection uses Apple’s system picker and does not give the app access to your entire photo library.

Notifications are requested only when you enable processing notifications and are used to report processing success or failure, never for advertising. Camera access is requested only when you choose to capture a photo or video. Microphone access is requested when you record video with sound.

3. How We Use Information

We use this information only to provide account access, online media processing, on-device editing, file saving, Premium access, troubleshooting, and security. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app advertising tracking.

4. Service Providers

Account and business services may be hosted by Tencent Cloud services. Subscription payments are processed through Apple’s StoreKit. At your direction, online media processing accesses the source platform identified by the link you submit. We transmit only the data required to provide each feature. Service providers that can access personal information must protect it to a standard no less protective than the one described in this policy. Each source platform’s own privacy policy applies to its data practices.

5. Retention and Security

Account and entitlement information is retained while your account exists. Sessions, diagnostic logs, and temporary processing files are retained only as long as needed for functionality, security, or troubleshooting, and temporary files are removed according to service policy. Passwords are never stored in plain text. Information may be retained longer where required by law.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You can disable Photos or notification access in System Settings. After signing in, you can permanently delete your account from the Account page. Deletion removes account details, sessions, and entitlement links. Transaction records that must be retained for financial, security, or legal obligations are disassociated from the deleted account and kept only for the necessary period. Apple manages its purchase records; deleting an account does not automatically issue a refund or cancel renewal. You can manage those options in Apple subscription settings.

To request access, correction, or deletion, or to ask a privacy question, email jyinaune@163.com.

7. Third-Party Content and Children

Process only content that you own or are authorized to use, and follow the rules of its source platform. The app is not designed to collect children’s personal information. Minors should use it with guidance from a parent or guardian.

8. Policy Updates

We will update this policy when features or data practices materially change and provide notice in the app or by another appropriate method.
